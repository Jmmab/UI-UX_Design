| **Prototyping and Animation**
--- 
- Smart Animate:
    1. The elements must have the same name;
    2. The elements must be inside a frame.

| **Organization, Layout & Sizing**
--- 
- Grids and Columns;
    - The Industry standard is usually 12 **
        <!-- also, a good "rule of thumb" number for the gutter/gaps is 24px -->
    - Obs. : when designing for Desktop, is recommended to use big margins
- Another good, commonly used _rule of thumb_, this time for sizing text, is: "No smaller then 16px. No bigger then 72px"
    <!-- 16px beign the value used by default by most web browsers -->
    - Of course, let's not forget: this is also important because of accessibility!! **

| **Dealing with assets**
--- 
- Boolean Operations:
    <!-- For vector manipulation. It's not Figma's "specialty", but is nuce to know -->
    - Union;
    - Subtract;
        <!-- Notes: * The order of the layers matter ** Useful when exporting -->
    - Intersect;
    - Exclude.
- You can also use 'Flatten' which is the _destructive_ option
- Usually it's good practice to manipulate things like images and other assets in specific software before bringing them into Figma;

| **Responsive Designs**
---
- Auto-Layout:
    - Hugging;
    - Constraints;
    - Nested Frames and Nested Auto-Layouts;
    - Autoheight.


`PRO TIPS:`
- You can copy things directly from the properties panel and paste them into a frame;
- Organize your colors into: 'Primary', 'Secondary' and 'Accent' colors;
    - Select light and dark versions of this colors, plus come neutral ones
    - It's a very common practice to use '_off-white_' and '_rich black_" instead of pure black and white
        <!-- subtle, but makes a difference -->
    - `!` "Detach Style"
        <!-- same goes for text -->
- Try searching for: ***Design Systems***, ***Style Guides*** and ***UI Kits***;
